## Description
Le Gardien du Savoir is a point-and-click quiz adventure. Play a young monk exploring a medieval world of towns and monasteries, talking to characters, collecting items and answering knowledge questions on science, history, geography and more. Each correct answer earns points and talents that unlock new places and story events. Learn as you go and become the Keeper of Knowledge. (French-language game.)

## Controls
Mouse: click or tap to interact, choose answers and navigate menus. Touch supported on mobile.
